# Field 1 is not the triple

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
License: Apache-2.0

Field 1 is the host:

$$
X = V(F) \subset \mathbb{P}^5.
$$

Term B is the triple

$$
\langle X,\;\gamma_{\mathrm{bad}},\;p\rangle
\qquad
p:\forall z,\;\mathrm{cl}\,z=\gamma\to\mathrm{False}.
$$

\(\mathbb{P}^5\) is the ambient of Field 1. It is not Fields 2–3 and it is not Term B.

The chain atom \(W=u^5v+v^6\) and \(F=W\oplus W\oplus W\) specify how that host is written. They still do not write \(\gamma_{\mathrm{bad}}\) or \(p\).
